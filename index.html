Mission

ClaimRunner makes the small claims process more understandable, accessible, and manageable. Rooted in our values to use responsible technology, empower individuals, and reduce the access to justice gap, we transform confusing procedures, paperwork, and deadlines into a clear, guided experience.

Background

Our team carries experience across product development, design, engineering, AI, law, and public-sector strategy. ClaimRunner inspired us because we've experienced the feeling of being wronged and underserved by the system. We passionately believe that pursuing one should not depend on a person's income or legal knowledge.

Common questions

Is ClaimRunner a law firm?

No. ClaimRunner is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice or representation. Tools are for educational, organizational, and administrative support only.

Who is ClaimRunner for?

Self-represented people navigating small claims, starting in King County, WA. Expanding to more jurisdictions in the future.

Will ClaimRunner tell me if I am going to win?

No. ClaimRunner does not predict outcomes, assess legal merits of cases, liability, or recommend legal strategy.

How does ClaimRunner use AI?

AI may help organize information you provide, reduce repetitive work, improve clarity, and prepare materials for your review. It does not create facts, make legal decisions, or replace professional legal advice.

How can ClaimRunner help me?

If you are currently going through, or expect to go through, the small claims process: ClaimRunner provides clear, personalized, and actionable guidance through an intuitive experience. We automate document generation (such as demand letters and small claims applications), organize your information, and help tailor your material towards your local court’s requirements. We offer support through the entire small claims life-cycle so you won’t have to worry about missing steps, incorrectly submitting materials, or questioning your options!
